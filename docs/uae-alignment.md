# UAE and Abu Dhabi alignment

Dhill is positioned as an enabling layer for autonomous ground and aerial systems operating outdoors in the Gulf. This page maps it to public strategies and lists what is **not** yet verified. No endorsement by any entity is implied.

| Strategy or policy | Public goal | Dhill relevance | Verify before external use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abu Dhabi Smart and Autonomous Systems Council (SASC) | Move autonomous pilots toward practical deployment (reported Feb 2026) | Deployments meet real heat and dust; Dhill supplies the operating decision | Exact wording and date of the directive |
| Abu Dhabi SAVI cluster | Hub for smart and autonomous vehicles | Covers environmental robustness for ground and air systems, complementing road focused programmes | Scope of SAVI for non road robots |
| UAE Strategy for AI 2031 | AI leadership, testbed ambition | Applied AI decision layer with open evidence | Which AI 2031 pillars cite robotics |
| UAE Net Zero 2050 | Decarbonise power, industry, transport | Schedules PV cleaning and inspection robots around heat and soiling | Whether PV cleaning robots are named in plans |
| Operation 300bn | Industrial GDP growth by 2031 | Uptime and safety planning for outdoor industrial autonomy | Current target figure |
| Midday outdoor work break | No outdoor work in direct sun 12:30 to 15:00, 15 June to 15 September | Same thermal logic that protects people sets robot windows; a safety complement, not a labour replacement | Current year dates from the ministry |
| Data law (PDPL) and hosting expectations | Personal data protection and localisation | Weather and telemetry are non personal; fully self hostable | Legal review for each deployment |

## Observed conditions cited in the plan
- UAE recorded about 51.2 degC in August 2026 and 50.2 degC at Sweihan (press reports).
- A published western UAE PM10 study reports a very wide range (single digits to several thousand micrograms per cubic metre).
- A 2026 DEWA study discusses PV cleaning robot performance and safety (trade press summary).
These are context for the problem, not inputs to the model.

## Not verified in this repository
- Any quoted official number of autonomous vehicles or robots expected by 2027 (press figures differ).
- Current dates and wording of the midday break rule.
- Whether Abu Dhabi entities have an open call that matches heat and dust operability.
- Novelty: a systematic IEEE Xplore and Scopus search for prior work on envelope decisions for Gulf field robots has not been done.
