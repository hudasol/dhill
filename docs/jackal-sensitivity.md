**Headline:** across these ranges, `motors.drive_efficiency` moves the calendar GO share the most, by 69.1 percentage points, and `power.dcdc_efficiency` moves the die peak the most, by 7.87 K. This is a property of the L0 model; only the measurements in `docs/field-test-kit.md` can say which range is right.

Reading: the two efficiencies move the GO share by similar amounts, and the ground heating coefficient barely moves it. At the low value of either efficiency no hour is GO at full scale. A check of January with the low DC-DC efficiency found every hour DERATE or PARK with the battery binding and a best scale of at most 0.79, while the thermal headroom at full scale was positive in some of those hours; the zero therefore comes from a battery limit other than temperature, not from heat alone.
