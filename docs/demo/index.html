SYNTHETIC weather and illustrative L0 robot profiles. These are not forecasts and not real robots. The decisions are precomputed by the Dhill engine.

Filled bar and circle mean GO, hatched bar and triangle mean DERATE, empty bar and square mean PARK. Missions last one hour at the start time shown. Local time is UTC plus 4 hours. Decisions come from a 64 sample Monte Carlo at the 95 percent risk quantile.
